The Founder

Niclas Hiller
Founder, Chairman & CEO

Download executive summary (PDF)

Niclas Hiller brings a rare combination of sovereign wealth fund discipline and private wealth management expertise to every client engagement. His career spans more than 25 years across the world's most demanding institutional environments — from building the External Asset Management programme at Norges Bank Investment Management (NBIM), manager of the Norwegian Government Pension Fund Global, to serving as Chief Investment Officer at Formue AS, one of the Nordic region's most respected wealth managers.

At Formue, Niclas developed and led a team of 30 investment professionals, growing assets under management from NOK 30bn to NOK 160bn. Under his leadership, Formue was recognised as the best Private Banking provider in Norway (Kantar/Prospera 2024), ranking first in investment competence and track record. He implemented a best-in-class institutional investment philosophy and built a centralised discretionary asset management business that grew from zero to over 30% of company AUM.

Educated at the Stockholm School of Economics (Advanced Degree in Portfolio Theory; CEFA Certified European Financial Analyst) and the Universität Zürich (Finance and Banking, Master Level), Niclas combines rigorous academic foundations with practical mastery earned across the Nordics, Switzerland, and the United States. He is also a member of the International Advisor Council at Sovereign Investors, the global network for leading sovereign fund practitioners and academics.

Niclas contributes through executive leadership, board and investment committee roles, and selected advisory assignments. He brings particular value to family offices, principal investment companies and institutions seeking to build or strengthen their investment capabilities—aligning portfolios, people and governance with the owner’s long-term objectives.
